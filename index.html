About Me

p>I am an undergraduate researcher at the University of Notre Dame working in theoretical and computational astrophysics. My research focuses on core-collapse supernovae, dense matter equations of state, neutron stars, and numerical methods.

Current Research

I study how the hadron-quark phase transition affects core-collapse supernovae using the GR1D hydrodynamics code. My work includes equation-of-state conversion tools, numerical stability improvements, and analysis of shock evolution.

I am also developing a neutron-star oscillation solver based on the TOV equations and Sturm-Liouville eigenvalue problem.
